Preserve source date/time basis; never timezone-shift a date-only range as if it were a UTC instant. An unknown/uncertain peak remains unknown, while a provisional outing can still be useful.

Use returned dated radiant samples when supported; a sampled range is not continuous visibility or a meteor-rate prediction. If radiant geometry is absent/outside model, say so and label general late-night guidance as general; do not apply an after-midnight rule to every shower. Watch a broad sky, not directly at the radiant. Keep Moon timing and impact uncertainty separate. For beginners, do not turn all-night darkness into an all-night beginner itinerary: offer a manageable optional session with breaks/return plan, explain its evidence and ask about comfort. A suggested duration is advice, not a calculated optimum.

Compare actual timestamped altitude/direction samples during darkness and supported Moon timing, explain low-altitude haze/terrain risk, and label a later practical suggested session as advice. Do not invent a universal minimum altitude or optimal threshold. The core proxy is not a whole-band model. For interval boundaries, requested-window is a calculation cutoff, not rise or set; missing boundary metadata is unknown. A horizon-crossing label is geometric, not apparent or terrain-aware. Sampled maxima are within the requested interval, not continuous best viewing. Planet, comet and eclipse plans retain their activity-specific geometry/contact predictions and source limits; unsupported targets need appropriate exposed ephemeris tools, not invented generic windows. Forecast samples cannot guarantee a clear session.

## Interpret evidence and answer a beginner

When a plan contains darkness, explain civil (-6 degrees), nautical (-12 degrees) and astronomical (-18 degrees) intervals as nested periods below the Sun altitude thresholds, clipped to the requested session window. They are not exclusive twilight phases. Matching intervals never establish polar night or fake/mocked ephemerides: a short evening window can already lie entirely below all three thresholds. Describe darkness during that session; do not infer the whole day's solar behavior from it.

Treat target geometry as an observing opportunity. Milky Way visibility is conditional on dark skies, clouds, Moon, transparency and a clear local horizon; above-horizon core geometry alone cannot promise a visible band or photograph. Saturn may appear to the naked eye as a point; its rings require a telescope and suitable conditions. An interval's direction is sampled at maximum altitude among the evaluated points, not a bearing for the whole window. Keep returned sample times and intervals; use a cited sky chart for changing on-site orientation.

Keep missing weather and access unknown. Distinguish an enabled provider that is unavailable from an unconfigured fixture/artifact/index and a query outside configured-source coverage. Unsupported seeing/transparency, a full deep-sky catalog and global coverage are separate capability/data gaps; adding workflow text does not supply them. Report the returned reason and continue with supported evidence. State local permits or restrictions only with a returned or verified official source; otherwise ask the user to confirm with the land manager. General travel preparation is advice, not evidence of local rules or safe access.
