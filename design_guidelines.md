# NightSky website design guidelines

Keep the sky as the visual anchor: Cosmic Black #0C0C0E, Midnight Blue #0E1633, Starlight White #F2F1EB, Meteor Gold #C2A977. Playfair Display headings, Inter body. Preserve the full-bleed hero and existing brand headline.

Wonder draws people in; practical, honest explanations help them continue. Activities and examples have different jobs. Use left-aligned editorial reading columns, one job per section, restrained borders and motion. No entrance gate, autoplay audio or deliberate page delays.

Home: hero, activity links, Understand/Explore/Compare/Prepare examples, photographic pause, reviewed meteors or evergreen preparation, closing action. Resources is searchable; News is attributed external reporting. The Collective is editorial with no form or signup. About contains no invented history. Expeditions remain retired.

Primary actions share verified website configuration. Missing public listing -> Get started. Sources and policy drafts remain noindex and block public release until verified.

Mobile: single header menu, no duplicate bottom bar. Keyboard focus, labelled fields, 44px touch targets, 16px body copy, reflow and reduced motion are required. Decorative video is omitted for reduced motion; text and actions remain readable with media blocked.
