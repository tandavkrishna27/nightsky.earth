It has not been re-enumerated into a new worldwide parent count. Do not present 373,619 as a current executable count after this overlay.

Any route must reread actual raw/decoded/ledger pins, retain nulls in exclusion masks, deduplicate identical overlaps and reject conflicts. Preserve the existing eight-owner overlay and all original/replacement identities when comparing to the legacy plan. Chunk-level transport may unavoidably contain already-retained hours or filler nodes: distinguish those physical bytes from new admitted ownership. They never become authority to overwrite, double-count or reacquire a retained source job. If the existing prohibition on physical duplicate transfer is to be relaxed for immutable shared chunks, it requires an explicit reviewed transport contract; this audit grants none.

Aggregation remains `dark-cloud-v1`: local observing nights 1991–2020, noon-to-noon local boundaries, Astronomy Engine 2.1.19, Sun altitude ≤ −18°, inclusive cloud thresholds 0.1/0.25/0.5, independent eligible-hour denominators, 90% completeness, at least 27 qualifying years and existing 80% bin-year/month-year guards. None of the reviewed sources documents a free service that executes NightSky's exact solar/timezone/threshold algorithm and returns sufficient immutable lineage. Local streaming aggregation is the practical design candidate: read each admitted immutable source block once, retain source identity and exact selected-hour masks, then process bounded cell/zone/time partitions. In-region cloud compute could reduce data movement but is a separate paid-resource/job decision, not work performed or authorized here.

Do not recompute solar eligibility once per destination (236,042) when the scientific identity is the cell/zone pair. Memory/work/output and denominators still need real contained measurement. Existing per-child/lineage/cache limits and baseline quality gates continue to apply.

**Efficient acquisition does not establish affordable aggregation.** A temporal store may remove queue delay and transport amplification; it does not remove the exact solar eligibility, timezone, completeness, threshold and lineage work above. Streaming bounds peak buffers, while total CPU time, scratch/output volume and serving fit remain unmeasured. No free compute or server-side NightSky aggregation entitlement follows from a free dataset licence.
