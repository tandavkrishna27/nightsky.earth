## Rights facts versus validator approvals

The grant, licence version, attribution, adaptation/database obligations and source-to-object chain are factual evidence requirements. Uniformly demanding `rights-review` and `admission-approval` receipts in addition to source/payload receipts is an **operator governance policy**, not a condition imposed by CC0, CC BY or ODbL. The current policy entails **1,756 role receipts** (429 × 4 + eight × 5), though shared evidence can support their subjects. The roles do not inherently require different people. Whole-source hashing invalidates every role when any source field changes, including unrelated notice fields; that can create avoidable repeated work.

Use objective, reusable evidence for exact grants/terms and reserve judgment for genuine conflicts, transformations and compatibility. An explicitly reviewed policy revision could reduce duplicated approvals while preserving pins, provenance and actual conditions. The existing validator cannot be bypassed by filling `approved` fields or generating its own trust file: it remains in force until deliberately revised. It proves receipt/pin consistency, not legal validity; its success is only `prepared-for-separate-acquisition-review`. **Do not auto-clear any source or call this packet a release.**
