## Source disposition and research boundary

The freshly re-read radiant study explains its distinction between nominal fit windows, full duration at half maximum and first/last detections. A fitted exponential has no finite physical zero-activity endpoint; selecting a cutoff requires a documented definition. Its source comparison profiles must not be relabeled as original coefficients. The retained public NASA flux/fluence header audit likewise supplies aggregate profiles, not per-code season columns. Neither route currently establishes full UTC seasons.

A future observational route through [GMN products](https://globalmeteornetwork.org/data/) would need pinned files/revisions, bounded size, shower assignment, quality cuts, missing-night correction and a season definition before acquisition or fitting. It is not required merely because other methods are incomplete. No observational dataset, software archive, ephemeris sample or provider/model run was acquired here. Public article reads do not authorize that acquisition. Individual factual fields need accurate provenance and scope; lack of a blanket article license is not by itself a prohibition on extracting an individual fact. Bulk tables, third-party material and source terms remain separate decisions.
