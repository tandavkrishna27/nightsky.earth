## Contract boundaries

The strict optional result array grows from two to at most five unique codes; existing two-row payloads still validate. Exact code/date associations prevent assigning one shower's range to another. ORI's date-only maximum cannot claim a UT basis; DRA's general period cannot claim explicit annual provenance. Published DRA UT wording and URS hour precision remain distinct from the old LYR/LEO minute wording.

References remain unfiltered context. Every maximum retains `maximumYear: 2026` when returned alongside another requested year; it must not be presented as that year's forecast. No civil endpoint is turned into UTC midnight. Neither a constellation direction nor the source's radiant image establishes numerical coordinates, epoch, drift, fit domain or local visibility. DRA/URS models and all-shower season-wide validation remain absent. IGN and NASA maxima are separate source predictions; no averaging or substitution is performed.
