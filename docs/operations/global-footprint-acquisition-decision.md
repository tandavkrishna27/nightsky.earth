The eight-parent proposal has a documented northern gap, unknown remaining seams, and no retained edition-bound polygons from which to prove closure. The proposed replacement is therefore a geographic-source decision, not a claim of complete observing-place discovery.

## Concrete preparation and acquisition boundary

The next small step is an identity/preflight packet for the single dated planet, followed by a separately reviewable body authorization only if it fits. This document proposes, but does not execute, at most **one HEAD** to the exact PBF URL and **one GET** to `https://planet.openstreetmap.org/pbf/planet-261012.osm.pbf.md5`, with the checksum response capped at 1 KiB and aggregate metadata response headers/bodies capped at 64 KiB. No redirects, retries, ranges, alternate editions or fallback mirrors. Absence, changed identity, missing exact length or exceeded allowance returns to review. Provider MD5 is transport corroboration; compute local SHA-256 during a subsequently approved body acquisition.

After those metadata facts and machine limits are frozen, the proposed body packet has **one GET**, transfer ceiling exactly the verified Content-Length `N`, and one retained raw file of exactly `N` bytes. **N remains unknown; there is no executable numeric body allowance today.** The rounded 89G listing must not be converted into a fabricated exact budget. The eight-continent 85,563,268,782-byte allowance does not transfer to this replacement. No continents, supplemental poles, new regional pilots or Overture queries are included.
