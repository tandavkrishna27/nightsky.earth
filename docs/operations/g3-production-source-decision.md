## What worldwide coverage still requires

Geofabrik distributes buffered, cascading geographic extracts, retains complete crossing ways/multipolygons, and describes `.poly` as its clipping boundary. These are not administrative borders; public PBFs omit contributor user/uid/changeset fields. See [technical documentation](https://download.geofabrik.de/technical.html) and the [download service](https://download.geofabrik.de/). None of those properties makes neighbouring parents disjoint or their union complete.

The [retained admission packet](global-catalogue-source-admission.md) inspected eight polygon snapshots and records a northern residual above **85.041770°**, an Antarctic rectangle from −90° to −60°, and split date-line parts in Asia, Oceania and North America. These are retained findings, **not polygons fetched, pinned or recomputed in this pass**. All other seam/island/ocean residuals remain uncomputed. The exact footprint sources to reconcile are `https://download.geofabrik.de/{africa,antarctica,asia,australia-oceania,central-america,europe,north-america,south-america}.poly`, individually expanded; these are metadata references, not an approved body manifest.

Before acquisition, pin edition-associated polygons and compute a date-line/pole-safe union and residual ledger against the agreed global geographic domain. Explicitly audit Greenland, Alaska/Aleutians, Caribbean, Pacific islands, dependent/overseas/disputed territories, and continental seams. Preserve the northern cap as a gap until evidence closes it; do not redefine global as selected populated countries. A residual can be documented without pretending it is searched, but an unresolved residual cannot receive completion credit for full global source coverage.

Use the retained eight-source order for deterministic overlap ownership and conservative full-cell footprints. Deduplicate OSM type/ID separately, retaining every lineage and quarantining version/geometry conflicts. Parent/child nonduplication does not remove parent overlaps. Candidate bounds, administrative polygons and crossing objects cannot stand in for extraction footprints. No retained Monaco/Faroe source is added to this eight-parent edition.

Named nodes and complete ways retain existing category/name precedence; relation-only and unnamed objects remain excluded. Incomplete ways, rejected date-line/polar means and unknown access need explicit exclusion/quality records. Source coverage means the policy was applied to the source, not that every park or observing location exists in OSM. The [retained integration](g3-retained-catalogue-integration.md) measured only 84 candidates, with 28 unknown memberships and no real extraction footprints. Its 3,688,899-byte catalogue and roughly 968 MB sampled peak RSS cannot be scaled into a worldwide size estimate.

The prepared 4,854 administrative features are already retained and reused by that integration; do not follow superseded statements in older packets that no boundary body exists. Their generalized geometry and unresolved identities remain limitations. Administrative coverage is a separate admission from OSM source footprint coverage.

## Licence and distribution decision

Treat the selected OSM derivative as ODbL 1.0 and retain **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, the [OSM copyright link](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [ODbL text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), and edition/modification notices. ODbL sections 4.4 and 4.6 address public derivative use and the offer of machine-readable derivative data or complete alterations/method plus additional contents. The project chooses the complete exact derivative package as its practical compliance route; the licence does not demand that users download the entire upstream planet.

Before public use, name the durable offer URL, active edition, all reconstructable pages/indexes, hashes/bytes, availability/retention policy and accountable maintainer. A private build or a few query responses does not fulfil this release policy. Raw source retention and public derivative hosting are different storage decisions. No public offer was created here.

## Practical acquisition and storage decision

**The 85.56 GB is proposed raw transfer/build input, not the MCP server footprint.** Keep raw PBFs, reference resolution and sorting on the owner-controlled builder. Ship only the accepted immutable catalogue and required notices/offer assets to the eventual serving/distribution destinations. Final worldwide candidate count, catalogue bytes, directory size, derivative-download traffic, refresh cost and 2-vCPU/4-GiB host fit are **unknown**. The historical 10-GiB-per-serving-edition ceiling was a stop policy, not a prediction or host capacity fact.

Use the existing source-local SQLite producer and streamed catalogue builder, with one active job. Retain all eight raw inputs for the first reproducible build unless a separately approved retention/removal plan says otherwise. Sequential execution reduces concurrent lookup/scratch pressure; it does not reduce this retained raw sum. Do not count on deleting failures or relying on future redownload availability. The old pilot's 100-MiB limit is not the global producer's interface; do not broaden the pilot to process continents. The [current pipeline documentation](offline-global-source-pipeline.md) supersedes the older preparation packet's list of missing producer/supervisor interfaces.
