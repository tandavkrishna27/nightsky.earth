## Meteor information

Existing annual dates and Moon/visibility labels have not been verified. No dated prediction or rate is published in the new interface. Recognized legacy IDs render noindex archive notices; general preparation links to NASA and Resources. The approved evergreen fallback is used on Home/index until reviewed dated evidence is supplied.

## News

The read-only Spaceflight News API is normalized by the website. Local API smoke returned HTTP 200 with a real headline. Reporting has real publisher/date/original-link attribution. Article images and summary text are deliberately not displayed pending reuse-term review. This reduces preview content, not the ability to follow a story.

## Images and fonts

Existing stock photography and hero video are reused within this local preview; ownership/licensing confirmation is still a release dependency. Existing Google Fonts stylesheet retained (Playfair Display and Inter): these request font resources from Google. No new analytics or forms added. Hero video is about 21.4 MB; optimize for public delivery before launch, without replacing the approved sky composition.

## Editorial identity

Approved Home/About/Collective narrative is represented in websiteCopy.ts. No invented team, founding date, expedition origin, conservation funding, testimonial, waiting list or community membership claim. Contact uses NightSky.earth2026@gmail.com. Privacy and Terms now show an effective date and describe the current website data flows; source and media-rights release checks remain separate.
