## Field-level reuse decision

The project's [excluded-source policy](data-sources.md#excluded-source-patterns) holds reproduction of AMS/IMO calendars without a reuse grant/license. The [original scope](../product-scope-v1-v3.md#v1-sky-event-coverage) calls for a small selected visual-shower reference table; it does not supply source rights. The proposed normalized, repeated multi-field calendar use needs an explicit recorded rights assessment of its exact source and intended distribution.

There is also a narrower precedent: [the GEM month admission](calendar-source-expansion.md#admitted-field) records an isolated attributed calendar fact extracted from original narrative, with no copied sentence/table/media and no invented blanket license. That does not mean every fact needs permission, and it does not automatically approve a thirteen-row date/radiant compilation. Normalizing values or omitting prose does not itself answer compilation, third-party provenance or distribution questions. A distinct isolated-fact route could be assessed against an inspectable source and actual intended use; it is not approved here by analogy.

**Admission disposition:** no new IMO rows enter runtime. Seek an exact-source, field-specific rights decision supported by applicable terms and competent review, or a written grant from an authorized holder covering the intended use. A compatible grant resolves only the rights gate; it does not certify source precision, visual applicability, model validity or the complete meteor journey.
