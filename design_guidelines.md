# NightSky.earth Design Guidelines

## Brand Identity
**Concept:** Curated night-sky and meteor experiences reconnecting people with the cosmos  
**Tone:** Poetic, minimalist, reflective, premium  
**Design Mood:** Dark, cinematic, serene — where Earth meets the stars

## Color Palette
- **Cosmic Black:** #0C0C0E (primary background)
- **Midnight Blue:** #0E1633 (accents, gradients)
- **Starlight White:** #F2F1EB (primary text)
- **Meteor Gold:** #C2A977 (accent highlights, CTAs)

## Typography
- **Headings:** Playfair Display (elegant serif)
- **Body:** Inter or Manrope (clean sans-serif)
- **Accent Text:** Italic serif for quotes and poetic lines

## Layout Principles
- Full-width hero and gallery sections
- Clean vertical flow with generous whitespace
- Smooth scrolling with fade transitions and parallax motion
- Subtle ambient animations (twinkling stars, shooting meteors - use sparingly)
- Responsive navigation: Home | About | Experiences | The Science | Journal | Contact

## Page Structure

### Homepage
**Hero Section:**
- Background: Looping timelapse video of meteor shower or starlit desert
- Soft black gradient overlay
- Headline: "Where Earth meets the stars."
- Subheadline: "Journey to the darkest skies on Earth to witness the brightest moments of the universe."
- Primary CTA: [Explore Experiences] (Meteor Gold)
- Secondary CTA: [Join the Waitlist] (outline variant with blurred background)
- Visual touch: Subtle constellation line animations or slow-moving star field

**About Preview:**
- Title: "Born of Earth. Guided by Stars."
- Poetic copy about reconnecting with cosmos
- CTA: [Our Story]

**Experiences Highlight:**
- Title: "Journeys Under the Infinite."
- 4 experience cards: Meteor Nights, Cosmic Silence, Dark Sky Treks, Eclipse Expeditions
- Each with poetic microcopy and imagery
- CTA: [View All Experiences]

**Science Section:**
- Title: "The Universe, Explained Beautifully."
- Copy about celestial events and expert guides
- Interactive meteor calendar or live star map

**Gallery/Journal:**
- Title: "Moments Beneath the Infinite."
- Visual grid of long-exposure astrophotography
- CTA: [Read Our Journal]

**Join/CTA Section:**
- Background: Gradient from black to deep indigo
- "Ready to look up?" with CTAs

**Footer:**
- Minimal: Logo, navigation links, social icons
- Message: "Dedicated to the preservation of dark skies and the spirit of wonder."

### About Page
- Hero with poetic quote about looking up
- Brand story explaining NightSky.earth origin
- Mission section: "To preserve the night, and inspire humanity to look up again."
- Animated star field or Earth horizon at night background

### Experiences Page
- Elegant grid of experience cards
- Each card: Image, title, description, season/duration, CTA
- Full detail pages for each experience

### The Science Page
- "Why We Travel by the Stars" - celestial alignment explanation
- "Our Guides & Experts" - team introduction
- "Protecting the Dark Sky" - conservation and ethics
- Visuals: Minimal constellations, subtle parallax, soft glowing lines

### Journal/Gallery Page
- Grid of cinematic images with short stories
- Tags: Meteor Showers, Eclipses, Field Notes, Night Photography
- Fullscreen story view with minimal text and immersive visuals

### Contact/Book Now Page
- Minimal dark form: Name, Email, Message/Interest
- Side text: "The night sky belongs to everyone. Let's find yours."
- Contact: hello@nightsky.earth

## Imagery Direction
- Long-exposure astrophotography
- Desert, mountain, tundra, remote landscapes
- Human silhouettes under cosmic skies
- Cinematic, immersive compositions

## Interaction Design
- Smooth scroll behavior
- Fade-in transitions on scroll
- Parallax effects on hero sections
- Minimal hover states with subtle glow/scale
- No distracting animations - emotion first, subtlety always

## Priority
**Emotion first, information second.** Typography, spacing, and visuals should evoke serenity, awe, and connection with the cosmos.
