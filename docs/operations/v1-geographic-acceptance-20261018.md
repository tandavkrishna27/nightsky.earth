## Meaning of the classifications

- **Configured default:** available through configured API discovery without the two geometry opt-ins. This does not mean unconfigured startup has working accounts or calls a source automatically.
- **Opt-in:** implemented only with both `geometryEnabled` and `geometrySourceAccepted`; standalone startup reads these from `NIGHTSKY_GEOMETRY_ENABLED=yes` and `NIGHTSKY_GEOMETRY_SOURCE_ACCEPTED=yes`. This audit does not inspect or set them. Required source/quota/retention admission remains separate.
- **Unsupported/rejected:** a defined boundary, not permission to remove constraints, substitute a centroid, acquire a global catalogue or make a looser provider request.

All result labels describe the bounded configured source operation. `mappedFeatureCompleteness` stays **unknown**. A `searchCompleteness:complete` response means the bounded request did not reach its page/result/failure limits; it is not proof all real sites are mapped, accessible, dark or suitable.

## Exact current cardinality and geometry limits

`shared/nightsky/discovery.ts` allows five origins, five include IDs, five exclude IDs and **ten selected-region references**. The audit found an erroneous five-reference limit that made five distinct includes plus five distinct excludes unreachable. After coordinator approval, two regressions reproduced the schema failure; the one-line limit correction now matches the existing ten-region worker/output ceiling. Eleven references and more than five includes or excludes remain rejected. Repeated/shared IDs do not create additional regions.

Per origin: radius **1–300 km**, minimum **0–300 km** with minimum ≤ radius. Scope defaults are `originMode:any`, `regionMode:any`; a scope without a positive origin or included region is invalid. Top-level legacy `placeId`/radius cannot be mixed with explicit `scope`. API origin selections require `sourceRevision` and reject catalogue revisions. Region selections bind `regionId` plus exact source revision; a region label is insufficient.

POI requests use fixed `/v2/places`, **limit 20**, no pagination or retries. At most five positive candidate queries occur; `all` origins query only one circle. Public requested result limit is **1–10, default 5** (`shared/nightsky/places.ts`). Full upstream page, requested-result truncation or a failed/unknown member makes coverage budget-limited; absence of these does not prove real-world completeness. Categories are park, viewpoint, protected area, campground, beach and other (only mapped mountain/picnic tags for other); unknown tags do not satisfy other. Precedence handles overlapping types. Source links/Geoapify and OSM credits remain attached.

Geometry uses fixed `/v2/place-details` with `features=details,details.full_geometry`, one selected identity per request, conservative **two-credit** reservation, shared queue/quota and a separate **30-second** geometry stage followed by **15-second** candidate stage. Cold counts excluding prior resolution are **R + Q requests / 2R + Q credits** for R unique geometry references and Q positive query pages. Current bounds are R ≤ 10 and Q ≤ 5: at most **15 requests / 25 conservative credits**. The new integrated maximum-scope fixture verifies exactly ten geometry requests, five positive pages, ten receipts and twenty-five reserved credits while preserving every constraint. Cache hits can reduce dispatches but cannot justify a larger cold-path claim. These are ceilings, not latency or provider acceptance measurements.

`geometrySnapshot.ts` accepts one identity-matching `details` feature containing original Polygon/MultiPolygon, at most **262,144 UTF-8 bytes**, **1,024 total ring positions**, **32 total rings**, and conservative topology validation. Planar GeoJSON longitude/latitude segments use **1e-9 degrees** boundary ambiguity. Interior holes are outside; shell/hole edges unknown. Disjoint islands and already split antimeridian parts work. Unsplit ≥180° edges, polar vertices, self-intersections, touching/nested/overlapping components, degeneracy and other unsupported topology are rejected. No simplification, geometry repair, wrapping or centroid fallback is performed.

Membership is **snapshot-planar-v1 relative to the fetched geometry**, not a certified legal boundary or known administrative vintage. Source vintage remains unknown. Receipts bind region/source revision, geometry revision, fetched/expiry times, policy and source attribution. Boundary ambiguity or geometry expiry during candidate arrival withholds the affected candidate and makes the result partial. Complex real countries may exceed these bounds; the existing code is not evidence of every-country availability.
