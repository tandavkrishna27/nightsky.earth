## Category, geometry and provenance admission

Retain the six response categories: `park`, `viewpoint`, `protected-area`, `campground`, `beach`, `other`. Existing importer selection precedence is park (`leisure=park`), viewpoint (`tourism=viewpoint`), protected area (`boundary=national_park`, `leisure=nature_reserve`, or `protect_class`), campground (`tourism=camp_site|caravan_site`), beach (`natural=beach`). `other` is a compatible output category, not permission to broaden the OSM filter. Name policy remains name/name:en/official_name with existing cleanup. Only reviewed node/complete-way semantics may be carried into a global build; do not silently enable relation/area handlers or accept partial-way vertex means from a different importer path.

Reject/quarantine missing or invalid representative geometry, incomplete references, unreviewed date-line/polar way means, or absent type/ID/release/source/licence lineage. Store public point coordinates with geometry-policy revision, exact source identity and native vintage. A point is not an entrance, horizon, dark-sky designation, verified access, opening-hours assurance or timezone. Relation-only and unnamed sites remain excluded and mapped-feature completeness remains unknown. Retained Fiji's timeout does not validate its date-line points.

[Overture Places overview](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/) describes point destinations and names/identity/confidence/source fields. It reports removal of `categories` in September 2026 in favour of `taxonomy`/`basic_category`; the [taxonomy page](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/taxonomy/) still describes coexistence. Pin v2.0.0 schema and release taxonomy before building an adapter rather than relying on either unversioned example. Park, campground, beach and viewpoint-like destinations are plausible mapping targets, but exact labels/hierarchies and exclusions for all six product categories are **not admitted**. Protected-area coverage is not inferred from a destination point, and `other` is not a catch-all. A category crosswalk, per-source provenance, valid point geometry and duplicate/closed-place policy are required before selecting this alternative. No world place count is used as our subset count.
